CadTech Wall Framing PRO — Change Notes

1. Wall member detailing controls:
   - 1–4 header plies
   - 1–3 jack/trimmer stud plies per side
   - adjustable blocking row spacing
   - sheathing sheet quantity estimate
   - optional conceptual diagonal brace overlay
2. Wall geometry QA for invalid wall lengths/heights, member IDs/coordinates, opening bounds, opening head/sill issues, and missing plate/header records.
3. Wall member schedule CSV and wall QA CSV exports.
4. Selectable wall elevation SVG dialog.
5. Wall panels are divided into reviewable segments with opening counts.
6. Fixed CAD wall thickness inference for centerline-only lines. The short bounding-box dimension is used only when credible relative to the wall length.
7. Added regression test at app/test-wall-framing-pro.mjs.

Limitations: This is a framing layout/detailing aid. It does not certify structural adequacy or replace applicable code calculations for headers, studs, lateral systems, anchorage, connections, sheathing fastening or load paths.
