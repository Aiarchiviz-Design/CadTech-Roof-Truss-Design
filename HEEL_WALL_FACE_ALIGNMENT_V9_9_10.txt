V9.9.10 - raised-heel outside face flush with the exterior wall outside face
=========================================================================
Problem: where trusses bear on CAD wall outlines (WALL layer), the truss end node was snapped onto the wall's outside face, and the raised-heel
stub and bottom-chord end are centred on that node - so both stood half a stub width (44 mm for a 2x4) OUTSIDE the wall face. The heel stub and
the wall framing did not line up at the corner / eave.

Fix (3 files):
 * app/roof.js - every eave end that lands on a wall outside face is now recorded in truss.wallBearings (before: only ends that had to be moved,
   so with your Project.dxf - trusses drawn exactly on the wall faces - nothing was recorded). Each entry carries alignFace = the new setting.
   Jacks are recorded when they already sit on the face (they are not moved).
 * app/connection-geometry.js - for a flagged bearing a face plane is built at the wall outside face: the end stud is moved inward until its
   OUTSIDE face is on that plane (full width kept), the diagonal landing on it is trimmed to the stud's inner face, and the bottom-chord end (stub
   heel or sawn heel) is cut flush. The rafter / overhang tail is NOT touched, so overhangs stay as specified. Analytical nodes stay on the wall
   face (as everywhere in the app); only the physical solids change - so 3D view, truss drawings, take-off lengths and cut lists all agree.
 * app/index.html + assembly-app.js - new GENERATION option "Raised heel outside face flush with exterior wall outside face" (default ON).
   Untick it for the previous behaviour (heel centre line on the wall face). Click "Generate complete roof" after changing it.

Result on Project.dxf (70 trusses, auto-designed): 79 wall bearings; heel stub and bottom-chord outside faces were 44 mm outside the wall face,
now 0.0 mm for all 79. Trusses with no wall bearing are unchanged.

Test: node app/test-heel-wall-face-v99.mjs (uses "Example Impoting files/Project.dxf"; fails on the previous code).
Also: test-dxf.mjs rewritten for the strict TRUSSES-layer importer; test-takeoff-v99 chord bound widened (peak-mitered short chords are longer than their centre line).
Cache-busting: connection-geometry v20, roof v9, assembly-app v15.
Known, unrelated to this change: test-reference-connections-v96.mjs and test-reference-joint-style.mjs fail in the package as received (the peak-joint classification
they check changed in an earlier patch).
