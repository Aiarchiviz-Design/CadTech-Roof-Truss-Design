Added workspace: SHOP DETAILING + BRACING

Includes:
- Model-linked truss shop schedule with member counts/sections and available analysis reaction data.
- Bearing length requirement field and bearing review status.
- Girder-to-jack, heel-to-wall, hip/valley transfer, and bracing anchorage connection detail register.
- Temporary erection bracing checklist and permanent lateral-restraint / roof diaphragm review register.
- CSV export for schedules and a printable review package.
- Project, jurisdiction, reviewer, revision and bracing coordination inputs.

IMPORTANT ENGINEERING LIMITATIONS:
This is a preliminary coordination / review tool, not a certified engineering package. It does not produce a PE stamp, final shop drawings, validated connector plate or fastener designs, exact design reactions unless correctly analyzed and independently verified, or a jurisdiction-compliant bracing design. Bracing intervals are planning inputs only and must not be treated as prescriptive code requirements. Confirm applicable code edition and current BCSI guidance with the truss designer/engineer. Do not fabricate or erect from this package until a qualified engineer and truss designer have completed and approved the project-specific design, installation bracing plan, permanent bracing plan, bearing details, connections and load path.
