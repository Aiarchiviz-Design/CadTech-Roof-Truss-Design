AI TRUSS DESIGN STUDIO V10.3 — ENGINEERING QA / STANDARDS REVIEW WORKSPACE

Added to SHOP DETAILING + BRACING:
- ISO 9001-aligned project QA checklist (not ISO certification).
- Fields to record the applicable TPI standard/edition and SBCA BCSI edition; user must confirm the current applicable editions for their jurisdiction and project.
- Licensed engineer name/registration, review status and signed drawing/calculation reference fields.
- QA release checklist covering document control, design inputs, reactions/load combinations, bearing, girder-to-jack transfer, plates/fasteners, temporary bracing, permanent bracing, fabrication inspection and final release.
- Approval/standards metadata included in package preview, CSV export and print review package.
- Explicit HOLD status when signed approval reference is absent or engineer status is not marked approved outside the app.

IMPORTANT:
This is a coordination and quality-record workflow, not a certification engine. It does not verify ISO 9001 conformity, identify the legally applicable TPI/BCSI edition automatically, provide a professional engineer stamp, or independently validate reactions, connections, bearing, fabrication tolerances or bracing. A user-entered approval status/reference is a record only and is not cryptographic verification. Do not fabricate or erect until project-specific drawings/calculations and erection/permanent bracing plans are reviewed and signed by appropriately qualified professionals for the jurisdiction.
